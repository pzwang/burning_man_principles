# Beyond the Ten Principles: Summary

This summarizes
[`research/BEYOND-TEN-PRINCIPLES.md`](research/BEYOND-TEN-PRINCIPLES.md), which
holds the full argument, tables, and sources. Its model is a working
hypothesis, not a replacement for the Ten Principles.

## The Question

What social mechanisms produce what participants call "playa magic," and
which of them can carry over into durable communities and into settings
shared with AI systems? The research treats the Ten Principles as evidence
from a successful but contradictory experiment, not as axioms. It separates
values from norms, ideals from enforceable duties, practices from hoped-for
effects, and stated ethos from actual governance.

## Five Findings

### 1. The Principles describe a culture after the fact

Larry Harvey and the Regional Network team wrote the Ten Principles in 2004,
about eighteen years into the culture, to guide new regional events. They are
a retrospective compression of practices that already worked. The organization
calls them descriptive rather than prescriptive, an *orthopraxy* of shared
practice rather than shared belief. Copying the words without recreating the
material, spatial, ritual, and governing conditions is unlikely to reproduce
the effect.

### 2. The ten items are different kinds of things

The list mixes an entry condition (Radical Inclusion), an exchange practice
(Gifting), an anti-market boundary (Decommodification), a personal capability
(Self-reliance), an expressive permission (Self-expression), a mode of
production (Communal Effort), a civic duty (Civic Responsibility), an
ecological duty (Leave No Trace), a meta-practice (Participation), and a
desired state of consciousness (Immediacy). Treating them as equal items hides
how they cause, constrain, and sometimes contradict one another.

### 3. Enforcement is uneven, and what can be counted tends to rule

Some principles are enforced by law, permits, placement, and eviction. Others
are peer norms or private aspirations. A patch of debris is attributable and
auditable; purchased insulation, outsourced labor, and lack of presence are
not. The research names this **asymmetric detectability**: what institutions
can count and attribute tends to become the effective constitution.

### 4. Playa magic is an emergent effect of four conditions

The provisional model proposes four interdependent dimensions:

| Dimension | What it does | Failure modes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Protective membrane** | Separates participants from default scripts; open to persons, selective toward conduct; provides backstage space | Enclosure and purity / boundary collapse and capture |
| **Plural agency** | Keeps participants capable, different, able to refuse and leave; self-reliance as **capable interdependence**, including being capable of needing | Consumer dependency / atomized expressive sovereignty |
| **Reciprocal co-creation** | Turns capability into a shared world through gifts, participation, and communal effort | Passive consumption / compulsory contribution and status potlatch |
| **Attentional resonance and repair** | Embodied co-presence and mutual focus, with civic and ecological feedback when intensity becomes harm | Distraction and spectacle / conformity and frenzy |

> **Playa magic ≈ protective membrane × plural agency × reciprocal
> co-creation × attentional resonance with repair**

The multiplication marks bottlenecks: a near-zero in any one dimension can
collapse the effect. Serendipity, communitas, trust, and transformation are
possible results, not deliverables to optimize directly.

### 5. Much of what matters is left implicit

The Principles barely name several load-bearing conditions: separation from
normal time, finitude (a bounded place, duration, and ending), ordeal, ritual,
public spatial form, cultural transmission through camps and mentors,
receptivity, consent, privacy, governance and recourse, political economy,
and how people return and what persists. Some of these may travel better than
the named Principles.

## Extension to AI

"AI system" can mean a personal tool, an adaptive medium, an institutional
decision system, a delegated agent, a social participant, or infrastructure.
These differ morally and operationally. The core claim is:

> **The medium, model, metric, recommender, and agent are participants in the
> feedback loop, even when the interface presents them as neutral tools.**

Responsibility remains with designers, deployers, operators, and governing
institutions. Machine participation must be legible before its status can be
debated coherently.

The research proposes ten candidate commitments to test, not adopt:

1. **Legible agency:** people know whether they face a human, model, bot, or
   hybrid.
2. **Bounded memory:** forgetting and contextual separation are capacities,
   not defects.
3. **No relational substitution by stealth.**
4. **Convivial augmentation:** tools leave people more capable together, even
   when absent.
5. **Attention is not fuel:** engagement is a cost or diagnostic, never the
   goal.
6. **Protect unoptimized life:** spaces with no capture, scoring, or training.
7. **Rate-limit superhuman asymmetry** in generation, surveillance, and action.
8. **Full-stack Leave No Trace:** compute, energy, water, data labor, and
   dependency count.
9. **Human recourse and responsibility** for consequential machine action.
10. **Polycentric control:** local norms within shared rights and appeal.

Two further warnings shape the constitution work:

- **The serendipity trap.** A system that selects "spontaneous" encounters in order to
  produce predicted effects will end up managing openness rather than protecting it. It's better
  to create clearings: unallocated time, mixed proximity, safe refusal, and
  incomplete scripts.
- **Goodhart risks.** Optimizing proxies for gifting, participation,
  inclusion, community, or immediacy can ultimately corrupts them: swag, busywork, tokenism,
  forced sociability, purity theater. Enforcement is itself a feedback system,
  so due process, local judgment, graduated sanctions, and sunset clauses are
  a substantive part of the process, not merely administration.

## Where It Leads

The research's compressed hypothesis:

> A protective counter-context admits heterogeneous, capable actors; asks them
> to make a common world through non-equivalent contribution; concentrates
> embodied attention on one another; and closes material and social feedback
> loops through care, repair, and accountable limits.

The [pillars](PILLARS.md) document builds on this model.  Additional [essays](essays/) add distinct lenses: gift and
receiving, fire and finitude, ritual, and the plurality of places.
